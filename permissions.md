# Sound asset permissions

The sound effects in this folder come from Kenney's **Interface Sounds** pack and FrogPog's **Chiptune SFX Pack** on OpenGameArt. Both sources label their packs **Creative Commons CC0**. CC0 dedicates the work to the public domain to the extent possible; where that dedication is not effective, it provides a broad license to use, modify, and redistribute the material, including commercially. Attribution is not required, but credit is appreciated. This is a license summary, not legal advice.
